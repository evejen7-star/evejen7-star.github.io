What We Do

Six Core Services, Start to Finish

Six core services covering every stage of a commercial demolition or strip-out project, from initial site assessment through to final waste removal.

Commercial Demolition

Full and partial demolition of commercial, industrial and retail buildings, carried out under strict RC38/CDM regulations with structural surveys completed before work begins.

Soft Strip & Internal Demolition

Removal of non-structural elements - partitions, ceilings, flooring, fixtures and services - ahead of refurbishment or full demolition, with minimal disruption to surrounding units.

Office & Retail Strip-Out

End-of-lease and pre-fit-out strip-outs for offices, shops and retail units, returning the space to shell condition and meeting landlord dilapidation requirements.

Enabling Works

Asbestos removal coordination, services disconnection, temporary works and site preparation completed before the main contractor or next trade moves in.

Site Clearance

Clearance of vacant plots, demolition arisings and vegetation to leave the site ready for redevelopment, groundworks or sale.

Waste Management

Licensed waste carriage and segregation on site, with material recycled wherever possible and all disposal documented for your compliance records.
